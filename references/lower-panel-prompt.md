# 下半幅生成规范

只生成下半幅。将花括号替换为观察结论，不把整个模板原样传入。

```text
Use case: compositing / photographic material sculpture.
Input image: authorized source photograph; reference for subject identity and photographic texture.
Output: one landscape 3:2 lower panel ONLY, not a complete poster, no upper photograph.
Main subject: {subject}. Preserve {recognition features}, most of its complete silhouette,
original photographic surface detail and colors. Do not replace the subject with background scenery.
Physical transformation: {one material transformation and one coherent action}.
Make the source-derived photographic subject a tangible thin photo material object:
visible fine edge thickness, subtle lift/curl/fold, coherent perspective, realistic cast shadows
and contact shadows. Keep it recognizably the same subject, never a cartoon or generic substitute.
Backdrop: bright warm ivory paper with subtle texture and generous continuous negative space.
Composition: object about 55–70% of panel width, comfortably inside all edges; no clipping.
Doodles: {0–2 tiny black line figures and exact physical contact/action, or none}.
Text verbatim: "{short original English aside}". Small casual handwritten black lettering,
placed in clear paper space below the object, safely inside margins.
Style: authentic photography, restrained handmade collage, independent magazine editorial.
Avoid: flat sticker, generic torn-paper template, living duplicate replacing material object,
illustrated photographic subject, unrelated decorations, advertising, dense elements, extra text.
```

例如展翅鸟类可将完整鸟形变成小人撑起的薄片；建筑可作为被推开的折页；水纹仅在水面本身是主体时作为可铺展薄膜。示例用于说明判断，不应强迫所有照片复用同一构图或文案。
